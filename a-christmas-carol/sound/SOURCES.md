# A Christmas Carol — sound sources

Built by `build-carol.py`. Instruments from **Versilian Studios, VSCO 2
Community Edition** (CC0; credit gladly given: *orchestral samples by
Versilian Studios, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell*). The tune
*God Rest Ye Merry, Gentlemen* is traditional and in the public domain.
