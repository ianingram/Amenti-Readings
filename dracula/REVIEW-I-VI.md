# Dracula I–VI — speech attributions to confirm

Every quotation the cutter found, with the voice it was given. A wrong attribution is silent — it renders, it sounds right, and the wrong character has said the line — so read down the voices, not the words. Quoted words and titles that are not speech (“paprika hendl,” “Lenore,” “Marmion”) stay in their narration and are listed as such.
