# Dracula, Chapters XIII–XVII — speech attributions

Each document is read by its writer; a principal character inside a journal or diary speaks for himself or herself; anyone else — the undertaker’s woman, the solicitor Marquand, Dr Vincent, a carter — is spoken by the document’s writer. Letters, telegrams and memoranda are read whole. The Westminster Gazette cuttings at the end of chapter XIII are read whole by the Correspondent, the newspaper voice of chapter XI. **Every line in these five chapters was read and set by hand** (*confirmed*); *tag*, *letter* and *named* lines agreed with the automatic pass.
