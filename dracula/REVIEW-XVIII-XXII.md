# Dracula, Chapters XVIII–XXII — speech attributions

Each document is read by its writer; a principal character inside a journal or diary speaks for himself or herself — Renfield included; anyone else — the attendant, Thomas Snelling, the deputy at Korkran’s, Sam Bloxam, the house agent — is spoken by the document’s writer. A quoted title or word (“patient”, “drinking”, “soul”) is the writer’s. Letters are read whole by their writers; the house agents’ letter (XX) by Stoker. Every attribution not from an explicit tag was read and set by hand: **confirmed** where the automatic pass had it right, **corrected** where it did not.
