# The Count's lairs — map source

Base map: *Whitbread's New Plan of London, drawn from authentic surveys* (J. Whitbread, 1853).
Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division, https://www.loc.gov/item/2015591076/ —
free to use and reuse (no rights advisory). Credit: Library of Congress, Geography and Map Division.
Fetched as four IIIF quarters at 50% (`pct:0,0,50,50/pct:50` …) and stitched to 3821×2747.
Fonts: IM Fell English, Roman / Italic / SC (Igino Marini, OFL), from google/fonts.
Places from the text: Carfax (Purfleet, off the map to the east), 347 Piccadilly,
197 Chicksand Street (Mile End New Town), Jamaica Lane (Bermondsey); 50 boxes = 29 + 9 + 6 + 6.
Render: `python3 lairs.py whit50.png fonts out.mp4`, then crf 27 `-tune stillimage`.
