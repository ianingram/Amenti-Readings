## The score, Part One — built by `build-score-part-one.py`

Not a reconstruction of ancient music: a modern score that tells the story, a different
sound world for each kind of scene. All VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0; *orchestral samples
by Versilian Studios*): oboe, bassoon, F horn, tenor trombone, cello section (sustained and
tremolo), viola and violin sections, solo violin and viola pizzicato, upright piano, timpani,
tenor drum.

| file | the sound world |
|---|---|
| `score-opulence.mp3` | eastern, sensual — a drone on D, an ornamented oboe in the Phrygian-dominant scale, an oud-like pluck, frame drum in a slow 6/8 |
| `score-lygia.mp3` | spare and modern — piano in open arpeggios, a high string halo |
| `score-vinicius.mp3` | cinematic — a driving string ostinato in D minor, the cellos rising, timpani |
| `score-court.mp3` | heavy, unsettling — low brass clusters, a sliding bassoon, dripping pizzicato, a slow tread |
| `score-faith.mp3` | warm and still — a string chorale in D major, soft piano bells |
