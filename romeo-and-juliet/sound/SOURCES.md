# Romeo and Juliet — sound sources

Built by `build-sound.py`. Instruments: **VSCO 2 Community Edition** (CC0; credit: *orchestral
samples by Versilian Studios; organ sampled by Simon Dalzell of Ivy Audio*) — solo violin
(arco and pizzicato), viola section pizzicato, flute without vibrato, tenor drum, organ.

| file | made from | licence |
|---|---|---|
| `feast.mp3` | a galliard in D dorian, written for this production: "lute" (solo violin pizzicato), "recorder" (flute, no vibrato), viola bass, tabor | CC0 |
| `score-lovers.mp3` | the lovers' theme, written for this production: solo violin over a harp of viola pizzicato | CC0 |
| `score-organ.mp3` | a slow chorale on the VSCO organ, for the wedding and the dirges | CC0 |
| `verona.mp3`, `chamber.mp3` | *La fontaine de la place* (aldor) + *Turdus merula 2* (Oona Räisänen) + a church bell far off | PD |
| `garden-night.mp3`, `dawn.mp3` | *Common Nightingale's song 2* (Digweed1, CC0) + *Common blackbird singing* (tinga, CC0) + built crickets | CC0 / PD |
| `cell.mp3`, `tomb.mp3` | built stone quiet, drips and wind (*Howling wind*, CC0), blackbird outside | CC0 + synthesized |
| `passing-bell.mp3` | built: one deep stroke of a bell, for every death | synthesized |
| `knock.mp3` | *Knocking on wood or door* (stephan) | PD |

Borrowed by URL: the sword clash (Treasure Island), the dread (Dracula).
