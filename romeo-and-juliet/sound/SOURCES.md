## The swords — played as instruments (5 Oct 2026)

Built by `build-swords.py`, entirely synthesized; no recordings. Each blade is a tuned
steel bar (a free bar's modes, 1 : 2.76 : 5.40 : 8.93 : 13.34, each a beating pair),
ringing in the play's key — D, F, A and C.

| file | what it is |
|---|---|
| `sword-clash.mp3` | the broad clash: blade full on blade, D and A roaring, a four-second ring |
| `sword-parry.mp3`, `sword-beat.mp3`, `sword-wring.mp3` | tip on tip; a knock aside; steel sliding along steel |
| `sword-swish.mp3` | a blade cutting the air close past the microphone, the edge whistling on A, bent up as it comes and down as it goes |
| `fight-brawl.mp3`, `fight-brawl-2.mp3` | I.1: the servants' fray; Benvolio and Tybalt |
| `fight-mercutio.mp3` | III.1: Mercutio and Tybalt — quick parries, a long bind, the thrust |
| `fight-romeo-tybalt.mp3` | III.1: Romeo's fury, broad blow on broad blow |
| `fight-paris.mp3` | V.3: Paris and Romeo, the vault ringing |
