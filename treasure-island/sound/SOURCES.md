## The theme — "Fifteen Men" (4 Oct 2026)

`theme-fifteen-men.mp3`, 4:00, built by `build-theme.py`. The production's title
music and the landing page's sound; its parts are to be cut into score cues as
each Part is built (the horn call, the deck, the flute tune, the battle rise, the
drum corps, the coda adrift).

- **Words:** Robert Louis Stevenson, *Treasure Island* (1883), Chapter I — public domain.
  **Tune:** original, written for this production on Stevenson's stresses.
- **Instruments:** VSCO 2 Community Edition (CC0; credit: *orchestral samples by
  Versilian Studios, recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell*) — upright piano,
  flute, timpani, tenor drums, marching and orchestral snares, muted bass drum.
- **Built here:** the barrel kick's cask ring, the oar slap, the 808-style sub, the
  ship's horn and bell, the sea, the canvas, the round shot, the zips, the locks.
- **Recordings (Wikimedia Commons):** gulls — *Gull 1* (avphillips, PD), *Western Gull*
  (National Park Service, PD), *XC707075 European Herring Gull* (Sonothèque ADVL, CC0);
  *Knocking on wood or door*, *Creaky wooden casket* (stephan, PD); *Dull thud*
  (gregoryweir, PD); *Wine glass* (hugh, PD); *Howling wind* (CC0); and the gunfire
  and cannon from this folder.
- **Voices:** rendered through the Amenti voice service (Gemini TTS) — the crew's
  "Yo, ho", the verse, "All hands on deck!", the orders and the battle shouts. Each
  render is cached in R2 by its text, voice and style, so re-running the build asks
  for exactly the same takes and gets them back from the cache.
